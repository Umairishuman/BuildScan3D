\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Existing 3D scanning solutions for indoor environments require expensive LiDAR hardware or multi-step manual workflows inaccessible to construction site teams, facility managers, and small architectural firms. This project, \textbf{BuildScan~3D}, addresses the gap by providing an end-to-end pipeline that converts a commodity Android smartphone recording into a metric-scale, photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) model suitable for real-world measurements.

The system comprises five stages: (1)~a custom ARCore-based Android application that captures 30\,FPS video alongside camera poses, depth maps, and live image-quality feedback; (2)~a pose-prior module that aligns ARCore's OpenGL coordinates to the COLMAP/Nerfstudio convention; (3)~a cloud-GPU reconstruction pipeline that performs COLMAP Structure-from-Motion with Sim3 alignment to ARCore's metric frame, followed by Nerfstudio \texttt{splatfacto} 3D Gaussian Splatting training; (4)~a structural layer that extracts wall planes, corners, wall lengths and doors from the model as the basis for floor plans and 1:1 measurement (prototype built); and (5)~a planned web-based viewer for metric measurement and model inspection.

At mid-evaluation, Iteration~1 stands at \emph{core pipeline complete, refinement ongoing}: the Android capture app records all required sensor data with live quality and coverage guidance; the FastAPI processing server accepts uploads and dispatches jobs to on-demand RunPod GPU pods; and the current best reconstruction (v6) scores 26.52\,dB PSNR / 0.8886 SSIM on 42 held-out frames, and tape-measure checks show a mean measurement error of 4.8\,mm (0.19\%), worst case 0.29\%, against a 5\% accuracy target. Key technical challenges solved include IMU-bridged tracking-gap recovery, lens self-calibration via COLMAP, and diagnosis and correction of degenerate Gaussian splat geometry. The open items are 3DGS refinement, 3D mesh creation, completion of the structural layer, and the web workspace (including floor plans and 1:1 measurement), followed by end-to-end field validation.
