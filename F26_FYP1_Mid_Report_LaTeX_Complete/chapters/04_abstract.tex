\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Existing 3D scanning solutions for indoor environments require expensive LiDAR hardware or multi-step manual workflows inaccessible to construction site teams, facility managers, and small architectural firms. This project, \textbf{BuildScan~3D}, addresses the gap by providing an end-to-end pipeline that converts a commodity Android smartphone recording into a metric-scale, photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) model suitable for real-world measurements.

The system comprises five stages: (1)~a custom ARCore-based Android application that captures 30\,FPS video alongside camera poses, depth maps, and live image-quality feedback; (2)~a pose-prior module that aligns ARCore's OpenGL coordinates to the COLMAP/Nerfstudio convention; (3)~a cloud-GPU reconstruction pipeline that performs COLMAP Structure-from-Motion with Sim3 alignment to ARCore's metric frame, followed by Nerfstudio \texttt{splatfacto} 3D Gaussian Splatting training; (4)~a planned semantic segmentation layer using YOLOv8-Seg for MEP system identification; and (5)~a planned web-based viewer for metric measurement and model inspection.

At mid-evaluation, Iteration~1 is complete: the Android capture app records all required sensor data with live quality and coverage guidance; the FastAPI processing server accepts uploads and dispatches jobs to on-demand RunPod GPU pods; and the reconstruction pipeline produces models scoring 27.8\,dB held-out PSNR with metric accuracy within 0.3\% of ARCore's trajectory scale. Key technical challenges solved include IMU-bridged tracking-gap recovery, lens self-calibration via COLMAP, and diagnosis and correction of degenerate Gaussian splat geometry. Remaining work in FYP-2 includes semantic AI segmentation, the web measurement workspace, and end-to-end field validation.
