\chapter{Conclusion}

BuildScan~3D turns a casual video from a commodity Android phone into a metric-scale 3D Gaussian Splatting model of a room. At mid-evaluation, Iteration~1 stands at \emph{core pipeline complete, refinement ongoing}.

\paragraph{What we found.} Four findings shaped the design: (1)~image-based COLMAP poses replace ARCore poses for training: once ARCore's silent re-anchor and missing lens model were removed, training-view fit rose from 21.9\,dB to $\sim$28\,dB and held-out SSIM from 0.78 to 0.86; (2)~dense MVS initialisation is critical for splat geometry; (3)~aggressive regularisation degrades quality when combined with Adam; (4)~gyro-aware frame selection outperforms texture-based sharpness metrics.

\paragraph{What we built and measured.} A three-tier system---Android capture app with live IQA and coverage overlay, FastAPI processing server with RunPod dispatch, and a COLMAP + splatfacto pipeline with Sim3 metric alignment---with CI/CD via GitHub Actions. The current best model (v6) scores 26.52\,dB PSNR ($\geq$25\,dB target) and 0.8886 SSIM ($\geq$0.85 target) on 42 held-out frames. Tape-measure checks give a mean error of 4.8\,mm (0.19\%) on rigid objects and a worst case of 0.29\%, substantially better than the 5\% accuracy target. Processing currently takes about 75 minutes on an RTX 3070; the under-35-minute RTX 4090 target is projected and not yet measured.

\paragraph{Risks and next steps.} The risk register (Table~\ref{tab:risks}) tracks twelve risks; the highest are cloud GPU cost (cost per scan not yet measured), the unmeasured RTX 4090 processing time, tracking loss on featureless surfaces, and delivering the remaining FYP-2 scope. Mirrors and glass remain a known limitation. FYP-2 completes 3DGS refinement, the structural layer (wall planes, corners, wall lengths, door detection) with automatic floor plans, and the web workspace with point-to-point 1:1 measurement, and validates them on more rooms. 3D mesh creation is also in scope as planned work and has not yet been scheduled.
